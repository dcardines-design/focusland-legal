Focusland Privacy Policy

Last updated October 25, 2026

Focusland is a cozy focus timer for focusing alongside others. This page explains what information the app and our website use, and why. The short version: we only keep what the app needs to work, we don't show ads, we don't sell your data, and we don't track you across other apps.

What stays on your phone

Your tasks, coins, focus time and stats, unlocked rooms, looks, and settings are saved on your device.

What our server keeps

Friends, friend rooms and chat need a server, so Focusland uses Supabase to store a small amount of information. When you first set up the app, it signs you in as an anonymous guest (no email or password). Our server then keeps:

  • Your profile: the name you pick, your cat's color, your country (unless you choose not to show one), your streak and your friend code.
  • Friends: friend requests, your friends list, the friend rooms you host or join, and who sits in which seat.
  • While you focus: which room and seat you're in, when your round ends, whether you're on a break, and your first open task. This is cleared when you leave your seat. You can hide your task with "Show my task to others".
  • Chat: messages you send in a room or at your table. They are deleted automatically after 24 hours.
  • Reports and blocks: if you report a message, we keep a copy of it so we can review it. If you block someone, we keep that so their messages stay hidden for you.
  • Cheers: when you finish a task in a shared room, a short "finished a task" note is sent so gongs can ring. These are deleted after a day.

Who can see what

Your friends and the people in the same room can see your name, cat, country (if shown), your timer, and your task (if "Show my task to others" is on). Table chat is only visible to people sitting at that table, and room chat to people in that room. Some of the other cats you see in public rooms are simulated.

Sign in with Apple (optional)

If you choose to sign in with Apple, Apple gives the app an ID so your progress can be linked to you. Apple may also share your name and email, or a private relay email, if you allow it. We only use this to back up and restore your progress.

Purchases

Focusland+ and cosmetic packs are bought through Apple's App Store. Apple handles all payments. We never see your card or payment details. The app only checks with Apple which items you own.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, Focusland schedules reminders (like "Session complete" and streak reminders) on your device. They are not sent from a server.

App usage statistics

To learn which parts of Focusland people use and what to improve, the app sends usage events to PostHog, our analytics provider, for example "session started", "session completed" or "paywall viewed", along with details like the room, session length and coins earned. Each install gets a random ID, and PostHog uses your internet address to estimate your country. We don't send your name, your tasks, your chat messages or your email, and these events aren't linked to your Focusland account. Our website, focusland.co, also counts page visits and clicks with PostHog.

No ads, no tracking

Focusland has no ads, doesn't sell your data, and does not track you across other companies' apps or websites.

Children

Focusland is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from them.

Deleting your data

Go to Profile > Account > Delete account in the app. This removes your profile, friends, the rooms you host and your seats from our server, then wipes the app on your phone. Deleting the app alone only removes what's on your phone, so use Delete account first. Copies of reported messages may be kept for safety reviews. You can also email us to ask for your data to be deleted.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date at the top.
