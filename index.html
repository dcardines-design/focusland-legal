Focusland Privacy Policy

Last updated October 25, 2026

Focusland is a cozy app for focusing alongside other cats. This page explains what information the app uses. The short version: your focus data stays on your phone, we don't show ads, and we don't track you.

What stays on your phone

Your cat's name, color, country (if you choose to show one), coins, streak, focus time, tasks, unlocked rooms, looks and friend rooms are saved only on your device. We don't send this information to our own servers.

Sign in with Apple (optional)

If you choose to sign in with Apple, Apple gives the app an anonymous ID so your progress can be linked to you. Apple may also share your name and email, or a private relay email, if you allow it. This is kept on your device and is only used to back up and restore your progress.

Purchases

Focusland+ and cosmetic packs are bought through Apple's App Store. Apple handles all payments. We never see your card or payment details. The app only checks with Apple which items you own.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, Focusland schedules reminders (like "Session complete" and streak reminders) on your device. They are not sent from a server.

Other cats in rooms

Right now the other cats you see in public rooms and friend rooms are simulated. Your activity is not shared with other people. If we add real live rooms in the future, we will update this policy and the app before anything is shared.

App usage statistics

To learn which parts of Focusland people use and what to improve, the app sends anonymous usage events to PostHog, our analytics provider, for example "session started", "session completed" or "paywall viewed", along with details like the room, session length and coins earned. Each install gets a random ID. We don't send your cat's name, your tasks, your chat messages or your email, and these events aren't linked to your account or identity. Our website, focusland.co, also counts page visits and clicks with PostHog.

No ads, no tracking

Focusland has no ads, doesn't sell your data, and does not track you across other companies' apps or websites.

Children

Focusland is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from them.

Deleting your data

You can delete everything at any time from Profile > Account > Delete account in the app, or by deleting the app from your phone.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date at the top.
